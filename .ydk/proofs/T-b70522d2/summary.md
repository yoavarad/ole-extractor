Researched full NPOI retirement for .xls. Forked b2xtranslator to yoavarad/b2xtranslator (org-controlled), retargeted its Xls module to net8.0, fixed a real NUL-terminator bug found in the process, and ran an empirical fidelity spike against two real public-domain .xls fixtures from govdocs1. Sheet/cell-text conversion is solid, but b2x's Xls output writes zero document metadata (docProps entirely absent). Decision: .xls stays on NPOI HSSF -- NPOI's HPSF/POIFS roles for .xls remain required regardless of body-text library choice, so full NPOI retirement is not adopted. Findings recorded in docs/research/legacy-xls-parsing.md and docs/adrs/004-legacy-doc-ppt-parsing.md.
