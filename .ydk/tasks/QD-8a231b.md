Fix NU1903 (known high-severity vuln in System.Security.Cryptography.Xml 8.0.2, pulled in by vendored NPOI submodule's ooxml/openxml4Net/OpenXmlFormats modules) blocking the dotnet-quality pre-push plugin. These NPOI modules are unused by ExtractorOLE (only main/NPOI.Core.csproj for .xls HSSFWorkbook is referenced, per docs/project-rules.md) -- options: patch/override the vulnerable transitive package version, or exclude the unused ooxml/openxml4Net/OpenXmlFormats projects from whatever dotnet-quality builds (likely NPOI.Core.sln references them unnecessarily for our use case)
