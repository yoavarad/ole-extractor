## Description

Bring pptx's PowerPointFormatMetadata to full parity with ppt's shape (slide count, notes-slide count, macro-presence flag), sourced from OOXML Core/Extended Properties and presentation structure.


## Activity Log

### 2026-08-29 - PowerPointFormatMetadata parity implemented

Added `PowerPointFormatMetadata` DTO (SlideCount, NotesSlideCount, HasMacros) and
wired `PowerPointOpenStrategy` to populate it from the presentation part: slide
count from `SlideParts`, notes-slide count counting only slides whose
`NotesSlidePart` carries actual text content, and `HasMacros` from presence of a
`VbaProjectPart`. 4 new tests in `PowerPointOpenStrategyTests` cover multi-slide
with mixed notes, macro-enabled, non-macro, and null-not-placeholder-metadata
cases. Full suite: 89/89 passing, 0 warnings/errors.
